Beginner bookbinding, six evenings

Quillfold Studio runs a small-group course for people who have never sewn a book. Across six Thursday evenings you will make three finished books: a pamphlet-stitched notebook, a case-bound sketchbook and a Japanese-stitch album.

Details

  • Starts Thursday 15 January, 18:30 to 20:30, for six weeks
  • Eight places, so everyone gets time at the bench
  • Course fee: £180, which includes paper, board, thread and cloth
  • Optional second-term kit for the long-stitch binding: £35
  • Refunds up to seven days before the first session

What we'll cover

Folding and cutting signatures, reading paper grain, sewing with waxed linen thread, gluing up spines, and covering boards with cloth. Tutors demonstrate each step once, then walk round while you make your own.

Who it suits

No experience is needed, only a willingness to measure twice. Wear clothes that can take a little glue. We provide aprons, bone folders and needles, and tea is always on.

To book, email the studio from the contact page and say which evening block you want. Places are held for three days while payment arrives.
